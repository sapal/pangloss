# The Quantum Teapot

It was a cold morning in London, but Arthur didn't mind. He was looking at something **impossible**.

## The Discovery

"Pass me the sugar, would you?" asked *Elara*, her eyes fixed on the ceramic vessel.

Arthur hesitated. "It's floating, Elara. The teapot is literally hovering six inches above the table."

-----------------------

Elara laughed. "Minor detail! In the best of all possible worlds, tea serves itself."

**"But it's glowing blue!"** Arthur insisted.
