# The Quantum Teapot

It was a cold morning in London, but Arthur didn't mind. He was looking at something **impossible**.

## The Discovery

"Pass me the sugar, would you?" asked *Elara*, her eyes fixed on the ceramic vessel.

Arthur hesitated. "It's floating, Elara. The teapot is literally hovering six inches above the table."

-----------------------

Elara laughed. "Minor detail! In the best of all possible worlds, tea serves itself."

**"But it's glowing blue!"** Arthur insisted.

Before she could answer, three neighbors barged through the open kitchen door, drawn by the strange humming sound. "What on earth is that infernal racket?" demanded Inspector Barnes, clutching his heavy coat. "Look at the quantum telemetry!" gasped young technician Colin, waving a crackling sensor device. Mrs. Higgins peered over her spectacles with a calm smile. "Never mind the strange physics, dears, as long as it pours a decent cup of chamomile." "It is truly dangerous!" Arthur shouted, backing into the pantry. "No, it is a miracle," whispered Elara, reaching out a finger towards the porcelain spout.
